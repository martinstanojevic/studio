## Datasets

Browse classroom-ready datasets curated for teaching statistics and data science. All datasets are de-identified, cleaned, and accompanied by suggested activities.

### What you'll find

- **Real-world data** drawn from public sources — financial reports, consumer protection records, scientific studies.
- **Cleaned and de-identified** so they're safe to share with students.
- **Documented variables** with notes on encoding, missingness, and ethical considerations.

### How to use

Each dataset entry includes a description, the variables it contains, and a few suggested classroom activities. Pair a dataset with the relevant chapter from your CourseKata book, or use it for an open-ended student project.
