## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I adapt these materials for my own classroom?

Yes. All resources are free to download, modify, and adapt for your students. Attribution is appreciated but not required.

### Which CourseKata books do these resources align to?

Each resource lists the books it's aligned to (AB, ABC, ABCD, XCD) and the relevant chapter. Use the book selector on the Resources page to filter by your book.

### How are datasets de-identified?

All datasets are drawn from public sources or have been processed to remove personally identifiable information. Variables that could re-identify individuals are removed, generalized, or aggregated.

### How do I report an issue with a resource?

Email the CourseKata team or open an issue with the resource title and a description of the problem. We review reports weekly.

### Can I contribute my own resources?

We're always interested in classroom-tested materials. Reach out and we'll let you know what formats and metadata we need.
