## Overview

Each pair of students gets a bag of M&Ms, tallies the colors, and tests whether the observed proportions match Mars' claimed distribution. A reliable favourite — students engage with the data because they collected it themselves.

## Materials

- One fun-size bag of M&Ms per pair
- The worksheet (linked above)
- Calculator or spreadsheet

## How to use

Best after the chi-square lesson but before more abstract examples. Total time including bag-counting is ~60 minutes.
