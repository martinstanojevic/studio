## About this dataset

A cleaned, de-identified snapshot of romance-scam reports. Originally compiled from public consumer-protection complaints. Use it to teach categorical variables, missing data, and ethical considerations in publishing personal data.

## Variables

- `age` — age of the reporter at time of report
- `contact_channel` — dating site, social media, SMS, etc.
- `loss_usd` — financial loss in USD (can be 0)
- `outcome` — reported / pending / closed

## Suggested activities

Pair this dataset with a discussion of consent and anonymisation before analysis.
